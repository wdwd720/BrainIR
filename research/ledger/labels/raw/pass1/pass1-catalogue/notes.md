I read the bioRxiv full text. **No activation experiment.** CsChrimson-mVenus was used only to evaluate expression. I used it for name and line mapping only. ## Activation experiments cited by the resources
Interpretation of scope: the 2025 catalogue cites activation studies by behaviour only (no DN types named). Namiki 2018 cites a few with outcomes. Several cited studies are dedicated families of other sessions in `sources_seed.csv`: Cande 2018 (atlas), Bidaye 2014/2020, Braun 2024, Rayshubskiy, von Reyn 2014, Ache 2019, Hampel 2015 and Guo 2022. **I did not curate those, to avoid duplicating their sessions.** They remain leads for those sessions. I covered the cited activation studies that are not in the registry:
- Zacarias et al. 2018 (DNp09, SS01540): 1 record. There was an initial running bout and then freezing. I set the leg fields to `unclear` because the freezing convention (`no`) and the increased-locomotion convention (`yes`) conflict.
- Dombrovski et al. 2023 Nature: 8 records (DNp11; DNp04; DNp02; the DNp02+DNp04 combination line gives 2 records; the DNp02+DNp04+DNp06 line gives 3 records). The split-line IDs are only in Supplementary Table 2. The PMC/Springer supplement PDF could not be read (browser check / compressed PDF), so `driver_lines` = []. GF activation (>90% takeoff) is attributed in the text to refs 33–36, so I did not record it. Whether DNp02 was tested alone is inferred from "activation of DNp02 (including combinations ...)". Its takeoff-rate result is inferred from "Only DNp04, DNp11 and combination line activation increased takeoff rates significantly".
- Sen et al. 2017 Curr Biol (MDN unilateral activation): abstract only (paywalled), 1 low-confidence record. Effector kind is not stated in the abstract. I coded it `optogenetic` as a required-field placeholder; this is flagged in the record notes.
- Kohatsu et al. 2011 Neuron: abstract only, 1 low-confidence record. dTrpA1 activation of stochastic MARCM clones; courtship correlated with P1 and/or descending P2b. P2b maps to both pIP10 and DNxl053 in the kit, so it is UNMAPPED.
- Excluded, with reasons: Chen et al. 2018 (imaging only, no DN activation); Namiki et al. 2022 Curr Biol (DNg02 screen, tethered flight only, legs never described); Ros et al. 2024 Curr Biol (DNae014/DNb01/VES041, tethered flight only, legs never described); Ding et al. 2019 (paywalled; abstract names no DN type and has no leg information); von Philipsborn et al. 2011 (paywalled; abstract gives no DN-specific activation outcome).
