## S2. The third animal: build, gate, type matching and sides

  commit pinned in the ledger, checked by SHA-256; their run configurations read from their Zenodo record.
- **Build.** The matrix as released; every row single-signed, agreeing with the transmitter rule (verified, else predicted) for all
  4,863 rows the rule covers; sizes from surface area; excitatory descending neurons by the authors' rule (922); read-out the 139
  front-leg motor neurons (12 neck motor neurons left out, as in the other animals).

- **Type matching.** 1,221 of 1,313 descending neurons carry a MANC match ID; 860 excitatory ones map to 309 MANC types; high

- **Sides.** BANC's annotated sides agree with each neuron's output side for 62.7 % of 840 neurons (bar 90 %), so output sides are used; 232 types have copies on both sides, 181 of them with a single BANC label (the set of the left-right test).

## S4. The first literature test in detail

- **What it asked.** Whether the authors' whole-type score ranks types that drive rhythmic front-leg movement when switched on in flies above types that do not. 39 labelled types (25 yes, 14 no), labels frozen before scoring.
- **Verdict (locked).** Area under the curve 0.577 (0.417-0.724): not better than chance by the pre-registered rule. The best wiring baseline, picked on these labels, counted signed two-step paths (0.669).
- **Why we do not interpret it.** All 21 types whose labels rest on one optogenetic atlas of intact,
  freely walking flies are "yes"; the "no" labels come from other kinds of experiment (takeoff, flight), so a type's label is
  confounded with its source. Most labels describe intact flies, whereas the model is of the nerve cord alone.
- **Audit.** Fresh readers re-read every source: 3 errors among the 39 types (7.7 %), 0 among the atlas types and 3 among the 18 others; below the pre-registered 10 %, so the test stands, with an erratum for six types excluded because their outcomes were known (DNg100's label corrected to yes).
- **Re-scored with audited labels (exploratory, once).** Simulation 0.559; two-step counting 0.713.
