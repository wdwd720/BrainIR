# Notes — session pass1-escape_landing-r0-01 (pass1)

Sources: only the six assigned sources, read from their local copies in `sources/`. I did no web access. I did not look for or see any model, simulation, rhythm score or driver call, so there is no `sightings.csv`, and `saw_simulation_score` is false on every record.

## Per source
- **10.1016/j.cub.2025.11.035 (Croke et al., DNp03)**: 3 records. The local copy has the full text and legends, but no figure images or videos, so I read the legends and the text only. All activation readouts are wings, head or body during flight, and the legs are never described, so the leg fields are `not_assessed` (section 3, wing/flight convention). This paper uses only SS01559. Line specificity is set to `unknown` because the paper does not discuss off-target expression.
- **10.1038/s41586-022-05562-8 (Dombrovski et al.)**: 19 records. The paper reports every DN line it tested with CsChrimson in FlyPEZ (Extended Data Fig. 1e and source data; Supplementary Tables 1–2), so I coded it as `systematic_screen`, with one record per line × DN type. Fig. 1e–i shows the same experiments, pooled by cell type. I folded those panels into the per-line records rather than writing duplicate per-figure records. Takeoff and postural lean are single movements, so the legs are coded `no`. I gave the no-effect lines (DNp03, DNp05, DNp06) `medium` confidence: the observation window is short (50 ms pulse, about 100 ms tracked), though posture is tracked automatically.
  - **Mapping conflict**: the paper names SS02612 as DNp05, but `driver_lines.csv` lists SS02612 as DNp07. I kept the paper's name, set `mapping_confidence` to medium, and proposed the row in `proposed_driver_lines.csv`.
  - SS49010 and SS49024 (DNp11) are missing from the kit; both are proposed.
  - For SS49051, the hemidrivers in the paper (R18G08 AD; BJD100H09 DBD) differ from the kit (GMR_18G08-x-VT017682). The DN type is the same.
- **10.1038/s41593-019-0413-4 (Ache et al.)**: 5 records. The paper mentions a 133-line activation screen in tethered flight, but it reports results only for DNp07 and DNp10. Because the other lines are not listed, I could not write no-effect records for them. A later session could look for a supplementary list of those lines. SS01549 also labels DNp13 weakly (Supplementary Fig. 1b), so I wrote a separate `multi_dn` record for DNp13. The paper attributes the effect to DNp07. Fig. 2j–k (graded leg extension vs. estimated spike rate) uses the same lines as the non-flying activation experiment, and its flight state is not stated, so I merged it into those records. The videos were not available locally.
- **10.1098/rspb.2024.1724 (Chai et al.)**: no qualifying experiment; the GF manipulations are Kir2.1 silencing only. The electronic supplementary material (figshare) is not in the room, but the main text describes no activation.
- **10.1101/2025.10.28.684868 (Jones et al.)**: no qualifying experiment. The optogenetic activation targets somatosensory md/bristle neurons and ascending-neuron lines; no DN line is activated. The genotype table is an image, but the text version (supp/article.txt) lists no DN lines.
- **10.14814/phy2.12322 (Tschida & Bhandawat)**: no qualifying experiment. DA-DNs were activated only by current injection and TH-GAL4>UAS-P2X2 with ATP, which is chemogenetic. Section 1 lists only optogenetic or thermogenetic activation, so I wrote no record. The paper reports that DA-DN activation did not elicit leg movements. If a later pass decides chemogenetic activation should count, this would be a `no_visible_effect` record (TH-GAL4, so non-specific).

## Uncertainties
- The Croke flight records are included under the section 3 wing/flight convention, with legs `not_assessed`, even though section 1 asks for leg movement to be observable. Adjudication may want to drop them.
- In Dombrovski et al., the per-line posture effect is not shown separately (Fig. 1e pools lines by type). Each DNp02 or DNp11 line record carries the pooled-type posture result.
