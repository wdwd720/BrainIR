All four assigned sources were readable (full text). None yields a qualifying record, so `records.jsonl` is empty.

- **10.1016/j.conb.2023.102822 (Simpson 2024, review).** It summarises activation results from other papers, e.g. DNg12 anterior grooming, DNp09 freezing or walking, MDN, oDN1, GF. It reports no experiment of its own. Figure 1 is a schematic. Secondhand statements were not coded as records.
- **10.1016/j.cub.2022.01.008 (Namiki et al. 2022).** This is a CsChrimson activation screen of 50 lines that covers several assigned types: DNg02 (many lines), DNg03 and DNg07/08/09 via R42B02, DNg07 (SS01074, SS02551, SS02635), DNg09 (SS01058), DNg12 (SS02608), DNb06 (SS02631), DNp09 (SS01540), DNp02 (SS01053, SS02379), DNp10 (SS01049) and DNp11 (SS02379). All flies were tethered and flying, and only wingbeat amplitude and frequency were tracked. Legs are not observed or described anywhere: I checked the text, the figure legends, Fig. 1 and Table S1. PROTOCOL section 1 requires observable leg movement, and the task asks for experiments that report what the legs do, so I wrote no records. A different reading would code these lines as `wing_or_flight` with legs `not_assessed`. I chose not to, and an adjudicator may want to review that choice.
- **10.1016/j.cub.2024.04.003 (Yoshikawa et al. 2024).** The activated neurons are MGT and UMGT1, both VNC-intrinsic and not DNs. None of the assigned types is activated.
- **10.1038/s41467-018-06857-z (Chen et al. 2018).** This paper is calcium imaging only, of DNa01, DNb06, DNg10, DNg13 and MDN. There is no activation experiment. The supplementary videos were not downloaded, but the text, legends and supplement describe no activation.

No simulation scores, ranks or driver calls were seen in any source. No proposed synonyms or driver lines.
