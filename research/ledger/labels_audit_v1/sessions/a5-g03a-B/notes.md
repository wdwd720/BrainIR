# Notes — session a5-g03a-B (audit, run 5)

I read all four assigned sources: text.txt, the supplement text files and the figure legends. No source contains an experiment that qualifies under PROTOCOL section 1 for any assigned DN type, so `records.jsonl` is empty.

- **10.1016/j.conb.2023.102822 (Simpson 2024 review).** The review restates other papers' activation results, e.g. "Activation of DNg12, for example, evokes the alternation of head sweeps and front leg rubs", and its Fig. 1 lists DNp09, MDN, oDN1, DNa02, DNg13, DNg02, DNp06, DNp10, DNp01, DNp11, DNp02 and DNp13. These are secondhand summaries, so I wrote no records. The primary papers would have to be coded on their own.
- **10.1016/j.cub.2022.01.008 (Namiki et al. 2022).** This is a CsChrimson screen of 50 lines (Table S1). It includes assigned types DNg02 (many lines and R42B02), DNg03 (R42B02), DNg07 (SS01074, SS02551, SS02635), DNg09 (SS01058), DNg12 (SS02608), DNp02 (SS01053, SS02379), DNp09 (SS01540), DNp10 (SS01049), DNp11 (SS02379) and DNb06 (SS02631). Every fly was tethered and flying, and the only outputs were wingbeat amplitude and frequency. Neither the text, the legends nor the supplement mentions what the legs did. Under section 1 (leg movement must be observable) and section 3 ("Wing, flight...: legs not_assessed unless the legs are described"), these trials are not leg records. A reviewer who prefers to keep flight-screen entries with all legs `not_assessed` could recover them from Table S1.
- **10.1016/j.cub.2024.04.003 (Yoshikawa et al. 2024).** The activated neurons are MGT, whose cell body is in T3, and UMGT1, a VNC GABAergic 12B neuron. Neither is a DN. Supplementary Table S1 (the screen of about 100 GMR lines) and the videos were not downloaded, so I could not check whether any screened line labels an assigned DN. The main text reports no such result.
- **10.1038/s41467-018-06857-z (Chen et al. 2018).** This is calcium imaging only (MDN-1, MAN, DNa01/SS00731, DNb06/SS02631, DNg10, DNg13/SS02538, DNg16/SS01543). It contains no activation experiments.

I saw no simulation scores, ranks or driver calls for any type, and none of these sources contains a connectome simulation.
